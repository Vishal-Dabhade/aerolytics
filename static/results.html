How the models did on 100 engines they had never seen, scored against each engine's actual remaining life.

Official test scores

RMSE is the typical size of an error in cycles, with large misses counting extra. MAE is the plain average miss. R² is the share of the variation in remaining life that the model explains. Lower is better for the first two, higher for R².

Predicted against actual

One dot per test engine, at its last recorded cycle. A perfect model puts every dot on the dashed line. Predictions stop at 125, so dots for engines with more life left sit below the line.

Dots above the line are too optimistic: the model thinks the engine has more life than it does. Dots below are too cautious. In maintenance, optimistic misses are the costly ones.
